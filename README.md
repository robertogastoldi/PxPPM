# Clustering Clusters with PxPPM

## Abstract
In many scenarios, while performing statistical analysis, observations can be divided into homogeneous groups. Groups obey some similarity dictated by a modeling assumption, and heterogeneity is encouraged between distinct groups. However, groups can also be similar at a latent level.  

In this project we explore a model to perform informed clustering of clusters. Ideally, once observations are divided into clusters, those clusters are further grouped together on the basis of exogenous information through covariate measurements.  

The project is motivated by an astronomical study: we consider stars belonging to the same galaxy, for which we want to identify clusters related to distinct structural parts, namely arm and core regions. However, groups of stars belonging to different arms may share similar dynamics in the galaxy and should therefore be clustered together at a higher level.
